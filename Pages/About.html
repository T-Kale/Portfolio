About Me

My name is Kale Parnham, and I'm a mechanical engineering student building a multidisciplinary foundation across mechanical engineering, engineering physics, and computational mathematics. I'm developing strengths in simulation, theoretical & applied mechanics, and physics-driven modeling.

I'm particularly interested in systems that demand reliability, efficiency, and iterative refinement. My goal is to contribute to complex, high-performance engineered systems where precision and accountability matter.

What I'm Studying

My academic work centers on core mechanical engineering principles-statics, dynamics, thermodynamics, materials, and numerical methods. I'm expanding my studies to incorporate engineering physics and computational mathematics to deepen my analytical foundation.

I'm especially interested in understanding the mathematics beneath engineering tools, not just how to use them, but why they work and how to improve them. I'm also drawn to additive manufacturing technologies and space systems.

What I'm Building

Outside of coursework, I work extensively on personal and academic projects involving CAD, prototyping, and 3D printing. I treat projects as experiments in structured problem-solving and as a way to strengthen technical intuition.

Some Current Projects include:

- Building a radio telescope

- Designing and manufacturing competition robotics hardware.

- Developing a simulation tool that models gravitational and celestial dynamics.

Through these projects, I'm refining my modeling discipline, documentation practices, and analytical rigor.

What I'm Working Towards

Long term, I aim to work on technically demanding systems that require cross-disciplinary thinking and continuous improvement. I’m motivated by environments where engineering decisions carry weight and responsibility.
